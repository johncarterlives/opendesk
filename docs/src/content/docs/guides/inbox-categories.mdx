With Collab, an inbox can be split into tabs, like Gmail's: **Primary** for mail from people, and categories such as **Updates**, **Newsletters** and **Promotions** for the rest. New mail is sorted as it arrives. Local categories only change where mail shows in opendesk. For Google accounts, you can also choose **Sync with Gmail** to keep category changes in both places. Changing a category preserves inbox membership, unread state and labels.

The tabs sit above the inbox while an account in view is split, in the order of its categories. In **All inboxes**, the tabs of every split account are joined, each with its unread count. They follow the order most of those accounts share (the first account's when there is no majority), and a category only another account has comes right after the one before it in that account's own order.

To change the order of the tabs without opening settings, drag a tab left or right along the row, or focus it and press <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>shift</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>→</kbd> (<kbd>mod</kbd> is <kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> elsewhere). Primary always stays first. The new order is saved where the tab bar's settings button leads:

- **Your own account**, when it is the only split account in view. If it followed your team's or the workspace's categories, it now has its own copy of them in the new order. Choose **Follow** in the account's category settings to go back.
- **The workspace default or a team's categories**, for admins, when every split account in view follows that set. Everyone who follows it sees the new order.

Tabs don't move when the order isn't yours to change: for viewers, for Gmail's categories, when admins turned off **Members choose their own**, or when the tabs in view join several different sets.

To move a conversation to another category, drag it from the list onto that category's tab, or choose **Move to** in its menu. Drag one of several selected conversations to move them all. In **All inboxes**, a tab can be a category only some of your accounts have: the conversations whose account has it move, and the message that follows says how many stayed. When none of them can go there, the tab says so while you hold them over it. You can undo the move from the message that follows.

## Who decides

Admins set categories once for everyone, so nobody has to set up each person's inbox. An account follows the first of these that applies to it:

1. **Its own choice.** Whoever manages an account can choose its own categories, or turn them off, in the account's settings, unless admins turned off **Members choose their own**.
2. **Its owner's team.** A team can have its own categories. Someone in several teams gets the first team's, in the order admins set.
3. **The workspace default.**
4. **Nothing.** The inbox is one list, and the mail page offers whoever manages the account a short guided set-up.

A new member's accounts follow the workspace or their team from their first sync.

## Set up categories for everyone

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Inbox categories">

Open **Settings**, **Inbox categories** (under **Channels**, next to **Mail accounts**) and choose **Turn on categories**. You start from suggested local tabs: Primary, Updates, Newsletters and Promotions.

</Step>
<Step title="Make them yours">

Rename, recolour, reorder or remove categories, add one of your own, or choose **Suggest with Ollie** and say in a few words what the inboxes are for. Primary always comes first and may be renamed, for example to *Important*. A set has 2 to 10 categories.

Give each category a short description of what belongs in it. Ollie sorts by these descriptions.

</Step>
<Step title="Decide whether members choose their own">

With **Members choose their own** on, anyone can pick their own categories, or none, for the accounts they manage. With it off, everyone follows the workspace or their team.

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Only the mail a change can affect is sorted again, from inboxes of the last 30 days, and never a conversation someone moved by hand:

- **A new category**: conversations in Primary, to see whether they belong in it. Mail in your other categories stays.
- **A category renamed or described differently**: conversations in Primary and in that category, so mail can leave it when it no longer fits.
- **Primary's own description**: conversations in Primary.
- **A removed category**: its conversations, which need a new home.
- **Order, colours and Members choose their own**: nothing is sorted again.

</Step>
</Steps>

To give a team its own categories, choose **Add a team** under **Teams**. Drag teams to change their order. **Follow the workspace** removes a team's own set.

## Choose your own for an account

Open **Settings**, **Mail accounts**, the account, then **Inbox categories**:

- **Follow** the team's or the workspace's categories.
- **Sync with Gmail** for a Google account.
- **Choose my own** for this account.
- **Don't split this inbox**.

If your admins set the categories for everyone, these are locked, and you can still turn Ollie's sorting on or off for the account.

## Sync Gmail categories

Choose **Sync with Gmail** in an account's category settings or inbox setup. This uses Gmail's five categories: **Primary**, **Social**, **Promotions**, **Updates** and **Forums**. Moving a conversation here changes its category in Gmail. Changes made in Gmail appear here after sync.

Ollie and sender rules do not sort this account while Gmail sync is on. Existing local categories and rules are kept for when you switch back. **Newsletters** is a local category and is not part of Gmail sync.

When you turn sync on, opendesk refreshes all mail already stored here, including old imports. Large inboxes can take several sync passes. A failed category move returns to Gmail's observed category. If the connection needs attention, reconnect the account.

Gmail labels individual messages. For a conversation with different categories, opendesk uses the newest non-draft inbox message. Moving a conversation updates its existing non-draft messages, including those that have not been imported. Future messages keep the category Gmail assigns them.

Gmail's API does not expose which tabs you have enabled in Gmail. opendesk shows all five categories and leaves Gmail's inbox layout as you set it.

## How local mail is sorted

New mail in an inbox is sorted in this order:

1. **Sender rules.** Mail from an address, or from a whole domain written as `@example.com`, always goes to the category you chose. Add rules under **Sender rules** in the account's settings, or choose **Always for this sender** when you move a conversation.
2. **People you write to stay in Primary.**
3. **Gmail's own tabs**, for Gmail accounts. Mail Gmail puts in Promotions, Social, Updates or Forums goes to the category of that name when the inbox has it (Forums to Social), and Gmail's Primary to Primary when every category is a built-in one. Newsletters Gmail files under Updates or Promotions are left to the next steps while the inbox has a Newsletters category.
4. **Ollie**, when AI is on for the workspace and **Let Ollie sort new mail** is on for the account.
5. **Mail headers.** Mailing lists, bulk mail and automatic mail go to the built-in Newsletters, Promotions and Updates. Everything else stays in Primary.

Ollie prefers a category other than Primary. Primary is for mail that is important, needs your attention and doesn't clearly fit another category. Mail Ollie is unsure of goes to Primary only when it seems to need you, otherwise to the category it fits best. A conversation you answer moves to Primary, and one you move by hand stays where you put it. Sorting never archives, reads or moves mail at your provider.

Moving mail teaches Ollie. Each conversation you move to a category, from its menu, by dragging it onto a tab or several at once, becomes an example for that account: when Ollie sorts the account's new mail, it sees your recent moves and puts similar mail where you put it, not only mail from the same sender. Right after a move, the sender's other recent conversations that Ollie or the headers placed are sorted again with what it learned; conversations you moved yourself stay put. Sender rules still come first, and undoing a move forgets it.

## Privacy

Admins choose the categories but never see anyone's mail or how it was sorted. A category and a sender rule are seen only by whoever sees the account.

To sort a message, Ollie reads its sender, subject and first lines, nothing more, and keeps nothing but the category it chose. For the conversations you move by hand, the account keeps the sender, subject and first lines of your latest moves as Ollie's examples, used only to sort that account's mail and never shown to anyone. Anyone can turn off **Let Ollie sort new mail** for an account they manage, whatever admins set. Sender rules and mail headers still sort it.

Sorting with Ollie uses a small amount of AI per batch of up to 10 conversations. A workspace that brings its own OpenRouter or TypeSafe key in **Settings, AI** sorts on that key, at no charge on opendesk. See [Your own provider key](/guides/ai/#your-own-provider-key).
