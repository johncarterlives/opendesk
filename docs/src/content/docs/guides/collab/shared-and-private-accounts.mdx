**Private** means only the member who connected the account sees it, including its conversations. Workspace admins cannot see it either. **Shared** means that member and the audience chosen for the account can see it.

| Audience | Who sees the account |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Every workspace member, including people who join later |
| **Choose teams and people** | Members of those teams and the people picked |
| No teams or people added yet | Only the member who connected it, until someone is added |

Viewers can read. Agents and above who can see a conversation can answer, act on it, share it, assign it and comment.

## Change account sharing

The member who connected the account manages it. **Workspace admins can also manage shared accounts**, including one whose owner has left. Making an account private is the owner's choice.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the account">

Open **Settings**, **Mail accounts**, then the account.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose Sharing">

Under **Sharing**, choose **Private** or **Shared**. For a shared account, choose **Everyone** or **Choose teams and people**, then click **Save**. An admin can also invite a person by email from the audience picker; they get access when they join, not before.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/guides/collab/sharing" alt="Sales account settings with the Sharing section and its Private, Shared and audience choices ringed as 1" />

Changing a shared account to private turns off its Handling. Conversations shared or assigned on their own have their own access too, so review those separately.

## Share one conversation

Open a conversation and click **Share**. Choose **Everyone in the workspace**, teams or people, then **Save**. Those teammates can help with this conversation without seeing the rest of a private account.

**Stop sharing** removes that conversation's separate share. It does not remove access someone has through the account or an assignment.

## Assign a teammate

Use **Assign** on a conversation and pick the teammates who should handle it. Assignees can see and answer the conversation, even without access to the whole account, and find it under **Assigned to me**. Assignment does not change mail at the provider.

**Shared with me** brings together conversations shared with you. Your own sidebar can be arranged to keep the accounts and views you use close by: see [Your sidebar](/guides/sidebar/).

## Comment for the team

Click **Comment** below the conversation. Comments stay in opendesk and are visible to teammates who can see that conversation. They are never emailed to the sender. Type **@** to mention a teammate. Use the comment count to open the discussion.

Mentioning a teammate who cannot see the conversation shares that conversation with them.

When replying, use the **Reply / Comment** switch to choose between email and an internal comment. Check the selected mode before posting. See [Replying](/guides/collab/replying/).

<Callout type="note">
When a member leaves the workspace, their private accounts and mail are removed from opendesk. Shared accounts stay for admins to manage or disconnect. Mail at the provider is kept.
</Callout>
